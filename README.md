# Steal a Sword

A multiplayer Roblox game I've been working on using Roblox Studio and Luau.

The idea is simple: collect swords, bring them to your base, earn money, and steal swords from other players.

What started as a small project turned into one of the biggest games I've worked on so far, with multiple systems built around collecting, upgrading, and protecting your base.

## Gameplay

Players start with their own base and can purchase swords from a conveyor belt.

Each sword generates money over time depending on its rarity. Players can use their earnings to collect better swords, purchase equipment, and improve their progress.

The main challenge is protecting your collection while trying to steal swords from other players.

## Features

### Sword System
- Conveyor belt with randomly spawning swords
- Different sword rarities and values
- Sword collection and stealing mechanics
- Display stands for collected swords
- Passive income based on sword rarity

### Base System
- 8 player bases
- 80 sword display stands
- Base ownership and protection
- Laser barriers to prevent unauthorized entry
- Multiple floors for sword storage

### Economy & Progression
- Cash generation system
- DataStore for saving player progress
- Rebirth system
- Equipment shop
- Leaderboards for player rankings

### Equipment
- Speed Coil
- Gravity Coil
- Tung Tung Bat
- Energy Shield

### World
- Custom-generated map
- Sword factory and conveyor belt
- Custom lighting and decorations
- Spirit-themed environment
- Multiplayer gameplay

## Development

This project has gone through several changes, including experiments with different gameplay ideas, map designs, and sword systems.

Most of the development has involved building systems, testing them in Roblox Studio, fixing bugs, and improving the gameplay based on feedback from friends.

I'm still learning and experimenting with Luau while working on this project.

## Project Status

The game has been published on Roblox and tested with other players.

Development is ongoing, with more improvements and features planned.
